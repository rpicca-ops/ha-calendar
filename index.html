Home Assistant – Personal Calendar Integration

This is a private, single-household application. It connects the Home Assistant installation of its owner to the owner's own Google Calendar so that upcoming family appointments (for example the shared "Famiglia" calendar) can be shown on the home dashboard.

What the application does

  • Reads the events of the calendars that the account owner chooses to share with Home Assistant (title, date, time, location).
  • Shows those events on a private wall dashboard inside the owner's home network.
  • Does not offer any public service and is not distributed to third parties.

The application is used only by its owner and by family members who are personally known to the owner.
